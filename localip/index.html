How To Use Local IP file finder and "Server.py"

Step 1 - Download The App And Server.py (server.py needs a PC)

Step 2 - Open The App And Server.py

Step 3 - Look For A Folder In Your Downloads called "files" OR Drag A Folder To The Window

Step 4 - Place Any Image, Video, or Music Files You Want To Show Up

Step 5 - Go To The "Server.py" Window And look for the IP address

Step 6 - Enter that IP And PORT On your ROKU

Step 7 - Wait For It To Load And Then Try To Load Up A Video, Music File, Or Picture On The Roku

Step 8 - You Are Finished IF It Loads, But If It Doesn't, Press The "Star" button And Confirm The IP

Step 9 - Celebrate!

IF YOU CAN NOT INSTALL FROM STORE, USE LIFF-v1.0.zip IN THE DEVELOPER PAGE

How To Install With Dev Mode

Step 1 - Turn On Dev Mode

Dev Mode Combination - Home 3x, Up 2x, Right, left, Right, left, right

Enable Installer And Restart

Set password (I Recommend "rokudev")

Step 2 - Find Your LOCAL IP

Go To Settings > Network > About > IP Address

Step 3 - Go To That IP In Your Browser

Step 4 - Get LIFF-v1.0 And upload it

Step 5 - Install with ZIP

Wait For it To Say "install success"

Step 6 - Open LIFF On Your ROKU

Step 7 - Celebrate!
